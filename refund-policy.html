Refund and cancellation policy

How refunds and cancellations work for a digital ebook.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

In short: the ebook is a digital product that is available to download as soon as your payment is verified, so purchases are generally not refundable once access has been delivered. We will refund in the situations listed below.

Before you pay

You can close the payment window at any time. You are not charged and no access is created.

Cancelling after payment

Once your payment is verified and your access link is issued, the order cannot be cancelled, because delivery is immediate.

When we will refund

  • You were charged twice for the same ebook.
  • You were charged but we are unable to give you access to the ebook.
  • The file is damaged or unreadable and we cannot provide a working copy.

We may also agree to a refund in other cases at our discretion.

When we generally do not refund

  • You changed your mind after receiving access.
  • You expected guaranteed fluency or a specific result (we do not promise one).
  • The ebook or access link was shared or misused.

Payment taken but no access yet

Sometimes the confirmation email arrives a few minutes after payment. If it does not arrive, message us on WhatsApp with your Razorpay payment ID or order ID and the email you used.

How to ask for a refund

Message us on WhatsApp with your order ID or payment ID, the email used at checkout, and the reason. WhatsApp +917016249687

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method through Razorpay. How long it takes depends on Razorpay and your bank or payment provider, which we do not control.

Access after a refund

If we refund an order, your access to the ebook may be withdrawn, and you should not keep using the ebook.
