Why SaudiSeekho?

SaudiSeekho helps learners study the Arabic spoken in everyday Saudi life through a clear, structured plan instead of scattered notes.

What is Najdi Arabic?

Najdi Arabic is the dialect traditionally spoken in the Najd region of central Saudi Arabia, including the Riyadh area. It differs from Modern Standard Arabic, which is why a dialect-focused roadmap helps.
