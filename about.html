What the ebook is and how to use it.

What SaudiSeekho is

SaudiSeekho is a learning brand focused on practical Najdi Arabic for learners in India and around the world.

The ebook

“Speak Najdi Arabic in 60 Days” is a 64-page digital roadmap with learning activities for each day, Day 1 through Day 60, in English, Hinglish and Arabic.

Who it is for

Learners who want a structured daily plan for Najdi Arabic. Check the sample pages to see if the style suits you.

Why daily practice helps

Short, regular practice makes it easier to remember vocabulary and expressions than occasional long sessions.

What to expect

A guided plan and your own consistent effort. We do not guarantee fluency or specific results.
