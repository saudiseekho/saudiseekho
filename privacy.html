Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and who handles it.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. What we collect

  • At checkout: your name, email address, phone number and country, and any coupon code you enter.
  • Order records: an order ID, Razorpay order and payment IDs, amount, currency, payment status and dates.
  • Messages: anything you send us on WhatsApp.

2. What we do not collect

Your card number, UPI PIN or bank login details are entered in Razorpay’s payment window. We do not receive or store them.

3. Browser storage

The site stores only your light or dark theme choice in your browser. We do not currently run advertising or analytics cookies. Your ebook access does not depend on anything stored in your browser.

4. How we use your information

To create and verify your order, deliver the ebook, email your access link, answer support messages, prevent misuse, and keep records we need for our business and legal obligations.

5. Services that handle data

  • Razorpay: payment processing and its checkout window.
  • Cloudflare: runs our order and delivery service and stores order records and the private ebook file.
  • Resend: sends your access email.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts and i.ibb.co: the site loads fonts from Google and some images from an image host, so your browser contacts them and they can see your IP address.
  • WhatsApp: if you message us, WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy apply.

These providers may process data in other countries.

6. How long we keep data

Orders that are never paid are removed automatically after 14 days. Records of paid orders are kept for order, support and accounting purposes. We do not yet have automatic deletion for paid orders. You can ask us to review, correct or delete your information on WhatsApp, and we will act on requests where we are able to and the law allows.

7. Security

We use HTTPS, server-side payment verification and private file storage. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The ebook is intended for adult learners. Younger buyers should have a parent or guardian’s permission.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
